- Sender signs as "Ruan" ("Aqui é o Ruan"), not Juan. (Ribeiro, 17/10/2026)
- Human, casual tone, 2-3 short WhatsApp blocks max (7 was "too much"). Mark breaks with "---" in the draft card.
- No emojis.
- Proposals sent to prospects may omit the price page (Ribeiro removed it for Luiza Rabelo); review plaques are a standard model (blue Google), never "in the brand's colors".
**Why:** Ribeiro corrected the first drafts as too formal and with emoji.
**How to apply:** any outreach/follow-up text for clients; use writing_draft_v0 card.
